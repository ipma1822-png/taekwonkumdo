OFFICIAL FOUNDER PROFILE

Jeon Seong Kweon

Founder of Taekwon Kumdo

President and Chairman, World Taekwon-Geomdo Federation (WTKF)

About the Founder

Jeon Seong Kweon founded Taekwon Kumdo, combining aspects of Taekwondo and sword-based martial arts into a structured training approach. Through the World Taekwon-Geomdo Federation, he promotes martial arts education, instructor development, technical standards, and international cooperation.

Creation and Copyright Record

The copyright registration record for the compilation work Taekwon Kumdo Yeonmudo (태권검도 연무도) names Jeon Seong Kweon as its author. The recorded creation and first-publication date is August 1, 2009. The copyright registration date is April 22, 2010, under registration number C-2010-004281.

Korea Copyright Commission registration search (search by registration number).

This copyright record documents the registered work and its recorded dates; it is not, by itself, government certification of the founding of an entire martial art.

Education and International Development

WTKF is developing structured curricula, multilingual educational resources, instructor recognition and management, and future online learning and credential verification processes.
