TAEKWONKUMDOFor International Instructors

Taekwonkumdo is a structured martial arts education system that connects the dynamic movement of Taekwondo with disciplined sword principles. It is designed to be taught, documented, verified and shared internationally.

01
WHAT IT IS

More than a mix of two martial arts

Taekwonkumdo connects movement, distance, angle, timing and center through a structured Cross Principle. The goal is not to simply combine techniques, but to create a coherent training system.

A clear philosophy makes it easier for instructors to teach the same principles consistently across different countries.
02
WHY IT MATTERS

A new layer for your existing academy

Instructors can add forms, sword fundamentals, basic cutting patterns, seminars and instructor education while keeping the identity of their existing school.

It can expand the range of classes and educational content without requiring an academy to abandon its current program.
03
HOW TO JOIN

Start small, then grow with the network

Begin with official materials and basic training, continue with instructor education, and then explore branch or designated-school participation according to local needs and readiness.

The recommended path is gradual adoption: learn the standard first, then expand responsibly.
04
OFFICIAL VERIFICATION

Training, branches and credentials should be verifiable

The WORLD HUB connects instructor information, branches, educational resources, media and official verification so international partners can confirm registered information through one headquarters platform.

Trust grows when education, records and verification are connected to the same official system.

Teach locally. Connect globally.

Taekwonkumdo is built for instructors who want a structured martial arts program, an international network and an official system for education and verification.
