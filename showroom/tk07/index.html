INTERNATIONAL BRANCH · 40 SEC

Open a Taekwonkumdo BranchA clear path from interest to official registration

An international branch is not created by simply using the name. The recommended path is to understand the standard, submit an application, complete headquarters review, receive approval and remain connected to official verification.

01
ELIGIBILITY

Start with instructor readiness

Branch candidates should first understand the Taekwonkumdo philosophy, training structure and expected responsibilities of an official representative in their country or region.

The goal is to protect teaching quality and the identity of Taekwonkumdo before expansion begins.
02
APPLICATION

Submit your organization and instructor information

Provide your contact details, academy or organization information, martial arts background and the region where you wish to develop Taekwonkumdo activities.

A complete application helps headquarters understand your local environment and the appropriate level of cooperation.
03
HEADQUARTERS REVIEW

Standards and suitability are reviewed

Headquarters reviews the application, verifies relevant information and may request additional communication, education or documentation before any official status is granted.

Submission does not automatically mean approval. Official status begins only after headquarters confirmation.
04
APPROVAL & REGISTRATION

Approved branches enter the official network

Once approved, the branch can be recorded within the WTKF network and follow the official education, branding, reporting and cooperation standards applicable to its role.

The branch name, representative and status should match headquarters records.
05
OFFICIAL VERIFICATION

Trust continues after approval

Official branch information should remain verifiable through the WORLD HUB. This helps instructors, partners and members confirm that a branch is currently recognized by headquarters.

Verification is part of the system, not an optional extra. It protects both the branch and the global network.

Build locally. Stay connected globally.

Taekwonkumdo international expansion is designed to grow through responsible instructors, clear standards and verifiable headquarters relationships.
